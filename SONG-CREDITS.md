All eight modules pass the port's current song loader. The files
include IT and XM formats; `shuffle.xm` was extracted unchanged from the
source ZIP. They were chosen for their explicit license, genre and author
diversity, and loader compatibility, with user ratings and download counts
used only as secondary signals. They are
not claimed to be the historically most popular Impulse Tracker songs.
The Mod Archive pages identify the composers and list CC BY 4.0 for those
complete files. The OpenGameArt page explicitly offers `shuffle.xm` under
CC BY 4.0. Individual embedded sample sources were not independently audited.
The separate `revenge-of-the-cats.it` and user-created `untitled.it` are not
covered by this notice and should not be bundled without a separate rights review.
