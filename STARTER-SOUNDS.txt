Tessera Starter Sounds 1.0.0 - Full edition

41 preset files: 24 drum one-shots (.ITS) and 17 instruments (.ITI).
Bass: 4; synth: 4; strings: 3; keys: 6.

The Full application archive already places the bank in samples/starter.
Extract the entire application ZIP, preserving its folders.
F3: press Enter to open the sample browser, then load a drum .ITS.
F4: press Enter to open the instrument browser, then load an .ITI.
No external samples, plug-ins, Python or synthesizer are required.

All sounds are original deterministic synthesis, mono PCM16 at 44100 Hz.
Melodic instruments contain four sample zones and a note map.
The names describe timbre inspirations, not recordings of real drum machines,
pianos, string instruments or branded synthesizers. No third-party PCM or ROM
recordings are included. Velocity layers and round-robin are not implemented.
Some drum presets share identical PCM; 41 files does not mean 41 unique sounds.

See samples/starter/CREDITS.txt and LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt in the Full edition.
PRESETS.tsv lists presets; manifest.json records original hashes and synthesis
provenance. Generator/source paths in the manifest describe internal provenance;
they are not required for playback and source code is not in the distribution.

The generator's showcase.it and audition.wav are omitted from the application
bundle, which contains exactly the two original example songs.
The application-only ZIP does not contain this sound bank.
