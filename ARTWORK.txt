TESSERA / dYNAX — portrait NFO edition

The GitHub README keeps the Tessera mark and live title text separate from the ANSI panels.
The remaining information panels are exact PNG sections rendered from a real ANSI file.
ANSI: UTF-8, 24-bit SGR colours, 79 columns. Use a fixed-width 1:2 font.
The two NFO companions are monochrome (UTF-8 and CP437).
The complete ANSI rendering remains available as assets/tessera-portrait/full.png.
